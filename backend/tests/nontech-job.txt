Position Summary
We are seeking a friendly, dependable, and customer-focused Customer Service Representative to join our Customer Experience team. The successful candidate will assist customers through phone, email, and chat while resolving questions, complaints, account issues, and product-related concerns.

Key Responsibilities
Respond to customer inquiries through phone, email, and live chat.
Provide accurate information about products, services, orders, accounts, and billing.
Resolve customer complaints professionally and efficiently.
Process orders, returns, refunds, exchanges, and account updates.
Document customer interactions and resolutions in the CRM system.
Escalate complex or specialized issues to the appropriate department.
Follow up with customers when additional assistance is required.
Meet established customer satisfaction, quality, productivity, and response-time goals.
Work collaboratively with billing, operations, sales, and technical teams.
Protect customer information and follow company privacy and security procedures.
Qualifications
High school diploma or equivalent required.
1–3 years of customer service or customer support experience preferred.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Excellent problem-solving and conflict-resolution abilities.
Comfortable working with CRM, ticketing, email, and chat systems.
Ability to multitask while maintaining accuracy.
Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.
Ability to remain professional and patient in challenging customer interactions.
Preferred Qualifications
Call center or contact-center experience.
Experience with Salesforce, Zendesk, or similar CRM/ticketing software.
Experience handling billing, orders, refunds, or account management.
Previous experience working in a high-volume customer support environment.
Success in This Role
The ideal candidate is empathetic, reliable, solutions-oriented, and comfortable helping a wide range of customers. Success will be measured through customer satisfaction, first-contact resolution, quality scores, productivity, and adherence to service standards. :::
